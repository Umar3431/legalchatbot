# ⚖️ LegalChatBot - AI Legal Assistant  

LegalBot is an AI-powered legal assistant that provides **legal information, advice, and official complaint applications**. It helps users understand **laws, legal rights, government rules**, and drafts **police complaints, FIRs, and legal notices**.  

---

## 🚀 Features  
✅ **Legal Advice:** Guides users on bail, legal defense, case procedures, and legal rights.  
✅ **Legal Applications:** Drafts structured **FIRs, police complaints, legal notices, and court applications**.  
✅ **Global Laws Support:** Answers legal queries from **any country**.  
✅ **User-Friendly & Professional:** Provides **well-structured, formal responses**.  

---

## 📌 Example Queries  
- **"How can I get bail for theft?"**  
- **"Write an FIR for a stolen mobile."**  
- **"How do I fight a false case?"**  
- **"Draft a legal notice for property dispute."**
